Original paste examples:

Changing sep = "" to sep = ",":

[1] "Leo,the,lion"

Explanation of sep:
The sep command controls what goes between items that are pasted together.
For example, sep = "" puts nothing between the items.
sep = "," puts a comma between the items.
sep = " " puts a space between the items.

Explanation of collapse:
The collapse command combines multiple results into one single string.
For example, paste(1:5) gives separate character values.
But paste(1:5, collapse = "") combines them into one value: "12345".

Extra examples:

paste(1:5, collapse = ","):
[1] "1,2,3,4,5"

paste(1:5, collapse = " "):
[1] "1 2 3 4 5"

paste("Number", 1:5, sep = "-", collapse = ", "):
[1] "Number-1, Number-2, Number-3, Number-4, Number-5"
